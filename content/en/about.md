      <p> <!-- callout? -->
        Brinsley is a <b>multi-instrumentalist</b> and <b>composer</b>. He has performed as a vocal soloist with the <b>BBC Philharmonic Orchestra</b> and played as <b>principal violinist</b> in multiple orchestras. <br><br> Brinsley holds a <b>First Class Honours BA in Music</b> from the <b>University of York</b>, where he specialised in Composition for Film in his final year of studies. Brinsley is also an alumni of <b>Chetham’s School of Music</b>, where he studied composition and singing alongside violin and piano.
      </p>

      <h1>University of York</h1>
      <p>
        Brinsley graduated in the summer of 2025 with a First Class Honours. At the university, his final year dissertation project involved creating a portfolio of newly written material scored to picture. (<a href="https://brinsleymorrison.com/blog/final-year-dissertation-project/" target="_blank">Read more about it here</a>). While studying at York, Brinsley also played with the <a href="york.ac.uk" target="_blank">University Of York Symphony Orchestra</a>, sang with <a href="york.ac.uk" target="_blank">The 24</a>,as well as commisioning music for and performed with <a href="york.ac.uk" target="_blank">The Chimera Ensemble</a>.
      </p>
      <p>
        In 2023, Brinsley was a choral scholar at the <a href="https://www.yorkoratory.com/st-wilfrids" target="_blank">York Oratory (Sir Wilfred's)</a> where he sang in a 4 part solo-voice ensemble, singing 2 services per week, and was also awarded a <a href="https://www.york.ac.uk/arts-creative-technologies/study/funding/choral-scholarship/" target ="_blank">Choral Scholarship by the University of York.</a>
      </p>
      <h1>Chetham’s School of Music</h1>
      <p>
       Brinsley went on to study at
       <a href="https://chethamsschoolofmusic.com" target="_blank">Chetham’s School of Music</a>
       for Sixth Form, where he was taught composition by <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/douglasmasoncomposer" target="_blank">Douglas Mason</a>
       and <a href="http://www.jeremypike.com/" target="_blank">Jeremy Pike</a> and had vocal tuition from 
       <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20110109012615/http://www.northernvoices.co.uk/html/helen_francis.html" target="_blank">Helen Francis</a>
       and <a href="https://g.co/kgs/U5eVBw" target="_blank">Marcus Farnsworth</a>.
       Brinsley also studied the violin and piano, and achieved his ABRSM Grade 8 on all three instruments before he finished his studies. Brinsley was part of jazz groups, orchestras, and even set up a barbershop ensemble- which he sang in, with weekly rehearsals and multiple performances. Brinsley was also part of other student-led choirs, as well as singing in Chetham’s Chamber and contemporary ensemble choirs.
      </p>

       Brinsley has been involved with the <b>BBC</b> on multiple occasions, performing on <b>BBC Radio 3</b> twice as a choral singer, firstly in the <a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b076b7td" target="_blank">Easter Sunday broadcast</a> from Hereford Cathedral, and secondly as part of the <a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b063dlsc" target="_blank">Three Choirs Festival service</a>.       <h1>Early career </h1>
      <p>
        Brinsley began his musical career in <b>Hereford Cathedral</b>, where he sang as a chorister for three years, from 2015-2017.
        During that time, he sang Bach's Passions and Oratorio each year amongs many other large musical works. During his time with Hereford Cathedral Choir, he performed in <b>Buckingham Palace</b>, on <b>BBC Radio 3</b>, and spent a week <b>on tour around America</b>. <br>
        At Hereford Cathedral, he sang 21 hours each week, and performed music in services 6 days a week. Although the workload tiring at such a young age, Brinsley felt incredibly rewarded and his love for music quickly developed as his vocal skills did too.
      </p>
