Brinsley performed with the University of York Symphony Orchestra in the 2022/23 season finale, given with the University of York Choir in York Minster as part of the York Festival of Ideas. John Stringer conducted Elgar's *The Dream of Gerontius*, with Joshua Ellicott as Gerontius, Kitty Whately as the Angel and Alexander Ashworth as the Priest and the Angel of the Agony.[^1]

## Programme

**Edward Elgar** The Dream of Gerontius, Op. 38

**Conductor:** John Stringer · **Soloists:** Joshua Ellicott (Gerontius), Kitty Whately (Angel), Alexander Ashworth (Priest, Angel of the Agony)

![The nave of York Minster during a University of York concert](/static/images/minster-jun-2023-1.webp)
*Photo: YorkConcerts*

Reviewing the performance, Steve Crowther praised the expansive orchestral Prelude and called Ellicott "imperious" in the title role.[^2]
