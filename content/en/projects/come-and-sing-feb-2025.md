Brinsley took part in this Come and Sing afternoon in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, in which singers from outside the university joined members of the University of York Choir and The 24 to work on choruses from Bach's Mass in B minor with Robert Hollingworth. The session ran from half past one until six and ended with an informal sing-through, open to listeners, in preparation for the full performance of the Mass the following month.[^1]

## Programme

**J. S. Bach** Choruses from the Mass in B minor, BWV 232

**Conductor:** Robert Hollingworth
