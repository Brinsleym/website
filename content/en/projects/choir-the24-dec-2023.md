Brinsley sang with The 24 in this Christmas concert in Central Hall, where the choir joined the University of York Choir and the University Baroque Ensemble under guest conductor Sarah Latto, with Asuka Sumi leading. Bach's *Magnificat* in D marked its 300th anniversary, set against Chiara Margarita Cozzolani's 1650 *Magnificat Primo*, sung by The 24, and a group of carols to close the term.[^1]

## Programme

**J. S. Bach** Magnificat in D, BWV 243  
**Gaetano Maria Schiassi** Concerto in D, "Pastorale per il Santissimo Natale"  
**Chiara Margarita Cozzolani** Magnificat Primo  
**Carols** Es ist ein Ros entsprungen · Praetorius Quem pastores laudavere · Puer natus · Vom Himmel hoch · Silent Night

**Conductor:** Sarah Latto · **Leader:** Asuka Sumi

![The University of York Choir, The 24 and Baroque Ensemble in Central Hall](/static/images/choir-the24-dec-2023-1.webp)
*Photo: YorkConcerts*

Martin Dreyer counted around 250 singers on stage and praised a crisp *Omnes generationes* and Latto's decisive conducting.[^2]
