Brinsley sang with The 24 for the November 2023 concert performing in The Jack Lyons Concert Hall.
The programme was conducted by Sarah Latto, and the programme details wer as follows:

**Clemens non Papa** Ego Flos Campi 
**Heinrich Schütz** Ego dormio 
**Raffaella Aleotti** Ego Flos Campi 
**Jean-Yves Daniel-Lesur** Le Cantique des Cantiques: 1. Dialogue 
**Samuel Coleridge Taylor** The Sea Shell 
**Philip Glass** Quand les hommes vivront d’amour

*Alongside guest conductor Sarah Latto, The 24 presents a vocal programme celebrating love in its many guises. The performance takes music inspired by the sensuous and striking text of the Song of Solomon from the Old Testament as its starting point, including sumptuous music by Victoria, MacMillan and Daniel-Lesur. Expect an evening of beautiful and thought-provoking poetry and choral music.*[^1]
