Brinsley performed with the University of York Symphony Orchestra in its autumn concert in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, conducted by John Stringer. Mel Bonis's tone poem *Le Songe de Cléopâtre* opened the programme, Alexa MacLaren was the soloist in Mendelssohn's First Piano Concerto, and the second half paired Busoni's *Berceuse élégiaque* with Strauss's *Death and Transfiguration*.[^1]

## Programme

**Mel Bonis** Le Songe de Cléopâtre, Op. 180  
**Felix Mendelssohn** Piano Concerto No. 1 in G minor, Op. 25  
**Ferruccio Busoni** Berceuse élégiaque, Op. 42  
**Richard Strauss** Death and Transfiguration, Op. 24

**Conductor:** John Stringer · **Piano:** Alexa MacLaren

Steve Crowther's review singled out Bonis, a composer he felt the concert revealed to be of real stature.[^2]
