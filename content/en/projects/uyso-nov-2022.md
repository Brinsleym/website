Brinsley performed with the University of York Symphony Orchestra in the first orchestral concert of the 2022/23 season, in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall. John Stringer conducted a programme of city portraits that travelled from Elgar's London to the Paris of Delius, Grainger and Gershwin. The concert sold out.[^1]

## Programme

**Edward Elgar** Cockaigne (In London Town), Op. 40  
**Frederick Delius** Paris: The Song of a Great City  
**Percy Grainger** Dreamery  
**Ravel arr. Grainger** La vallée des cloches  
**George Gershwin** An American in Paris

**Conductor:** John Stringer

![The University of York Symphony Orchestra performing in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall](/static/images/uyso-nov-2022-1.webp)

Tickets were "like gold dust", wrote Martin Dreyer, who described the orchestra as "a popular group" whose "standards are high". He heard violins that were "immediately bold" in the Elgar, a military march that "rang out with majestic bravado", and a Delius that reached "an immense climax, which suited the orchestra's mood perfectly".[^2]
