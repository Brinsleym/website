Brinsley sang with The 24 in the choir's spring concert in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, conducted by Robert Hollingworth. The programme ranged from Brahms' double-choir *Fest- und Gedenksprüche* and Gombert's Renaissance chanson to recent music by Joanna Marsh, Reena Esmail and Bernard Hughes, and ended with Howells' *Requiem*, whose solos were taken by singers from the choir.[^1]

## Programme

**Herbert Howells** Requiem  
**Johannes Brahms** Fest- und Gedenksprüche, Op. 109  
**Bernard Hughes** Precious Things · Perhaps  
**Kim Porter** Pulchra es et decora  
**Joanna Marsh** Batter my heart  
**Reena Esmail** TaReKiTa  
**Mark Edgley Smith** Love is more thicker than forget  
**Nicolas Gombert** J'ai mis mon cueur

**Conductor:** Robert Hollingworth
