Brinsley performed in The 24's autumn concert in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, conducted by Robert Hollingworth, for which the choir was joined by the choirs of Ampleforth College and Huntington School. The programme was sung entirely a cappella and ranged from Victoria, Gibbons and Johann Christoph Bach to Stanford, Elgar, Leighton and recent music by Joanna Marsh and William Campbell. Students from the MA solo-voice ensembles sang the Tomkins.[^1]

## Programme

**Tomás Luis de Victoria** Alma redemptoris mater  
**Orlando Gibbons** O clap your hands  
**Charles Villiers Stanford** Justorum animae  
**Joanna Marsh** All Shall Be Well  
**Robert Schumann** from Vier doppelchörige Gesänge  
**Johann Christoph Bach** Lieber Herr Gott  
**Edward Elgar** Go, song of mine  
**Kenneth Leighton** Drop, drop, slow tears  
**William Campbell** Come down, O love divine  
**Alonso Lobo** Versa est in luctum  
**Thomas Tomkins** Oft did I marle · Too much I once lamented

**Conductor:** Robert Hollingworth · **With:** Huntington School Choir, Ampleforth College Choir
