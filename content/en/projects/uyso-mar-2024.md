Brinsley performed with the University of York Symphony Orchestra in its spring concert in the Sir Jack Lyons Concert Hall, conducted by John Stringer. Anna Thorvaldsdottir's *Metacosmos* opened the evening, Charlotte Brettell was the soloist in Grieg's Piano Concerto, and the orchestra closed with Brahms's Fourth Symphony.[^1]

## Programme

**Anna Thorvaldsdottir** Metacosmos  
**Edvard Grieg** Piano Concerto in A minor, Op. 16  
**Johannes Brahms** Symphony No. 4 in E minor, Op. 98

**Conductor:** John Stringer · **Piano:** Charlotte Brettell
