Brinsley performed with the University of York Symphony Orchestra in the 2024/25 season finale in York Minster, where the orchestra was joined by the University of York Choir and The 24 under Robert Hollingworth. Brahms' *Ein deutsches Requiem* was framed by German Romantic choral music, including two pieces by Felix Mendelssohn and a prelude by his sister Fanny. The concert sold out.[^1]

## Programme

**Fanny Mendelssohn Hensel** Prelude to Lobgesang  
**Josef Rheinberger** Kyrie, from the Mass in E flat · Abendlied  
**Felix Mendelssohn** Warum toben die Heiden · Da Israel aus Ägypten zog  
**Johannes Brahms** Ein deutsches Requiem, Op. 45

**Conductor:** Robert Hollingworth
**Soloists:** Eleonore Cockerham (soprano), George Clark (baritone)
