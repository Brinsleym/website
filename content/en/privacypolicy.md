        <p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> January 17, 2025</p>

        <h2>Introduction</h2>
        <p>Welcome to <strong>brinsleymorrison.com</strong>. In this Privacy Policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to <strong>brinsleymorrison.com</strong>. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.</p>
        <p>By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions or concerns about how we handle your personal data, please contact us at <a href="mailto:contact@brinsleymorrison.com">contact@brinsleymorrison.com</a>.</p>

        <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
        <p>We collect the following types of information:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Contact Form:</strong> When you fill out our contact form, we collect the following personal information that you provide:
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Name:</strong> To identify you.</li>
                    <li><strong>Email Address:</strong> To respond to your inquiries.</li>
                    <li><strong>Message:</strong> The content you send to us through the form.</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <p>This information is processed by <strong>Formspree</strong>, a third-party service used to manage form submissions. Formspree will only store the data for the purpose of delivering your message to us and facilitating communication.</p>
        <ul>    
            <li><strong>Website Analytics:</strong> We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics platform, to collect anonymous usage data including:
                <ul>
                    <li>Pages visited and time spent on each page</li>
                    <li>Referrer website information</li>
                    <li>Browser type, device type, and operating system</li>
                    <li>Country and city location (based on IP address, which is immediately anonymized)</li>
                    <li>Screen resolution and preferred language</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
        <p>The personal information we collect is used to:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>Respond to your inquiries or requests submitted through the contact form.</li>
            <li>Understand how visitors interact with our website through anonymous analytics data to improve user experience.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>We do not use this data for marketing purposes or share it with third parties except for the purpose of processing form submissions via Formspree.</p>

        <h2>3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies</h2>
        <p>We use Umami analytics, which does not use cookies or store any personal identifiers. Umami collects only anonymous aggregated data and does not track users across different websites.</p>
        <p>The only other cookies that may be set are essential cookies needed to support basic functionality, such as session management during form submissions. These cookies are strictly necessary and do not collect any personal data.</p>

        <h2>4. Analytics with Umami</h2>
        <p>We use Umami analytics to understand website traffic patterns while respecting your privacy. Umami:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>Does not use cookies or store personal information</li>
            <li>Anonymizes all data before storage</li>
            <li>Does not track users across websites</li>
            <li>Processes but does not store IP addresses (they are immediately anonymized)</li>
            <li>Collects only aggregated, non-identifiable data</li>
        </ul>
        <p>You can prevent analytics tracking by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings or using browser extensions that block analytics scripts. For more information about Umami's privacy-focused approach, visit <a href="https://umami.is/docs" target="_blank">umami.is/docs</a>.</p>

        <h2>5. Data Storage and Security</h2>
        <p>Our website is hosted on a <strong>VPS server</strong> run by <strong>IONOS</strong>, a third-party hosting provider. While your personal data (submitted via the contact form) is processed by Formspree, we do not store any user data on our servers except for the contact form submissions. Formspree has its own privacy and data protection policies which you can review by visiting their website.</p>
        <p>Analytics data is processed and stored by Umami in compliance with privacy regulations. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information, including using <strong>HTTPS</strong> to encrypt data transmitted between your browser and our server.</p>

        <h2>6. Data Retention</h2>
        <p>We only retain your personal data for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected. In the case of form submissions, we will keep your data only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and engage in further communication. Once the communication is complete, your data will be deleted from our systems unless we are required to keep it for legal reasons.</p>
        <p>Analytics data is retained for a period of 12 months and is used solely for the purpose of understanding website traffic patterns and improving user experience.</p>

        <h2>7. Legal Basis for Processing</h2>
        <p>We process your personal data from contact forms based on your explicit consent when you submit the form. For analytics data, we process anonymous information based on our legitimate interest in understanding how our website is used and how we can improve it, while ensuring that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not overridden.</p>

        <h2>8. Your Rights</h2>
        <p>As a user, you have the following rights concerning your personal data under applicable data protection laws (such as the <strong>General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)</strong>):</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Right to Access:</strong> You can request access to the personal data we hold about you.</li>
            <li><strong>Right to Rectification:</strong> You may ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.</li>
            <li><strong>Right to Erasure:</strong> You have the right to request that we delete your personal data.</li>
            <li><strong>Right to Withdraw Consent:</strong> You can withdraw your consent at any time if you have provided personal data to us through the contact form. If you withdraw consent, we will stop processing your personal data for that purpose.</li>
            <li><strong>Right to Object:</strong> You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data for analytics purposes.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at <a href="mailto:contact@brinsleymorrison.com">contact@brinsleymorrison.com</a>.</p>

        <h2>9. Children's Privacy</h2>
        <p>This website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information as soon as possible.</p>

        <h2>10. Third-Party Services</h2>
        <p>We use the following third-party services:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Formspree:</strong> To process contact form submissions. Please refer to Formspree's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.</li>
            <li><strong>Umami Analytics:</strong> For privacy-focused website analytics. Umami processes anonymous data only.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Our website is hosted by <strong>IONOS</strong>, a third-party hosting provider that stores your form submission data. You can review IONOS's privacy policy on their website.</p>

        <h2>11. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>
        <p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any significant changes, we will notify you by posting an updated version on this page. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page indicates when the latest version of this Privacy Policy became effective.</p>
