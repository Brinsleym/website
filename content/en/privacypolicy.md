      <h2>Introduction</h2>

      <p>This Privacy Policy describes how Brinsley Morrison ("Brinsley", "I", or "we") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit and interact with this website (<a href="https://brinsleymorrison.com">https://brinsleymorrison.com</a>) ("Site").</p>

      <h2>Information We Collect</h2>

        <h3><strong>Limited Data:</h3></strong>
        <p>The Site does not collect any personal information about you through automatic means, such as browsing the website.</p>
        <h3><strong>Contact Form:</h3></strong>
        <p>The Site offers a contact form that allows you to send messages to Brinsley. When you use the contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and message (if you choose to write one). You are not obligated to use the contact form, and your use of it is entirely voluntary.</p>
        <h3><strong>Analytics Data:</h3></strong>
        <p>The Site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service offered by Google LLC ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies, which are text files placed on your browser, to help analyze how users interact with the Site. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the Site (including your anonymized IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. Google will use this information for the purpose of evaluating your use of the Site, compiling reports on website activity for Brinsley, and providing other services relating to website activity and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law, or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf. Google will not associate your IP address with any other data held by Google. You can opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics by disabling cookies in your browser settings.</p>

      <h2>Use of Information</h2>

      <p>Brinsley uses the information collected through:</p>

        <h3><strong>Contact Form:</h3></strong>
        <p>Brinsley uses the information you provide in the contact form solely to respond to your message. We will never sell or share this information with any third party for any reason, except as required by law.</p>
        <h3><strong>Analytics Data:</h3></strong>
        <p>Brinsley uses the limited data collected through Google Analytics to:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>Understand how visitors interact with the Site.</li>
            <li>Improve the user experience of the Site.</li>
            <li>Compile reports on website traffic and activity.</li>
          </ul>
      <h2>Formspree Integration</h2>
      <p>The Site utilizes Formspree (<a href="https://formspree.io">https://formspree.io</a>) to handle form submissions. When you submit a message through the contact form, your data is routed through Formspree's servers before being delivered to Brinsley's email address, contact@brinsleymorrison.com. Formspree has its own privacy policy, which you can find here: <a href="https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/">https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/</a>. Brinsley is not responsible for the privacy practices of Formspree.</p>

      <h2>Data Security</h2>

      <p>Brinsley takes reasonable steps to protect the information you provide from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no website or internet transmission is completely secure, and Brinsley cannot guarantee the security of your information.</p>

      <h2>Your Rights</h2>

      <p>Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:</p>

        <h4><strong>Right of access:</h4></strong>
        <p>You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.</p>
        <h4><strong>Right to rectification:</h4></strong>
        <p>You have the right to ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.</p>
        <h4><strong>Right to erasure ('right to be forgotten'):</h4></strong>
        <p>You have the right to request that we delete your personal data under certain circumstances.</p>
