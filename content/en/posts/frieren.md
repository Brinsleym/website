I undertook this project, to gain experience scoring to picture for anime. As I did not have any leads into the industry, I decided to rescore an existing anime as a hobbyist project. 

Sousou no Frieren was the choice I landed on because when I first watched it, I was captivated by Evan Call's scoring of the music. The music seemed to perfectly accompany the pensive and warm feeling that the storyline, voice acting and animation created.

My aim was not to create something "better" than Evan Call's original scoring. Instead, my goal was to follow in his steps, while creating something new and beautiful. I wanted to face similar challenges, and create new, beautiful material which it's primary purpose is to immerse the audience in the stunning visuals, storyline and voice acting.

I used AI to seperate the dialogue and music, using the free and opensource project [Ultimate Vocal Remover](https://ultimatevocalremover.com). While this is a tool designed for stem seperation, it also worked extremely well to seperate the speech, sound effects, and music from film.
After seperating all the elements I was ready to watch the episode with only its dialogue and sound effects.
